Six things that would sit inside the piece's own language rather than on top of it. Sketches are rough — text stand-ins for what the glyph field would render. Pick any, none, or the opposite.

00000000

gate handshake

The door currently just sits there. Instead it reads out one hexdump line that resolves into the word — the piece introducing its own grammar before you're in it.

~25 lines, gate only. No canvas work.
00000010

address gutter

It's called a hexdump and has no offsets. A very dim address column down the left edge makes the claim true, and gives the empty half of the frame something structural instead of nothing.

00000020

mode cycle

There are 25 render modes in PARAMS and the site shows one. A third word in the contact line cycles them. Costs nothing — the code already exists and is dead weight otherwise.

~8 lines. The modes are already written and self-tested.
00000030

pointer heat

PARAMS.lights exists, has two points, and is switched off. Bind one to the cursor: the field brightens where you are, so the piece answers you without any new machinery.

~6 lines. Move your pointer over the block above.
00000040

idle decay

Untouched for a minute, the wordmark loses cohesion — glyphs drift toward noise. Any input reassembles it. Rewards leaving the tab open, which is what a piece like this is for.

One lerp on the source's contrast + a mouse/key timestamp. ~12 lines.
00000050

now playing

Once the soundtrack is in, a low line under the contact words carrying the track — drawn as glyphs, so it reads as part of the field rather than a media widget.

~10 lines, reuses drawLinks' layout. Needs music.mp4 first.
